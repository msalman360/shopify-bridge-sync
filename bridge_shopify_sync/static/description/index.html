Shopify Bridge Sync

Odoo 18 Multi-Store Auto Sync

Connect your Shopify stores to Odoo and synchronize products, orders, and customers in real-time. Manage everything from one dashboard with automatic two-way sync.

Key Features

Product Synchronization

  • Import products with images, variants, and inventory
  • Auto-sync product updates back to Shopify
  • SEO fields: title, description, handle, tags
  • Variant management with SKUs and pricing

Order Management

  • Sync orders in real-time from Shopify
  • Cancel orders directly from Odoo to Shopify
  • Track fulfillment status and shipping details
  • View order lines, taxes, and discounts

Customer Sync

  • Import customers with full address details
  • Auto-sync customer information updates
  • Marketing preferences and tax exempt status
  • Multiple addresses per customer

Dashboard & Analytics

Visual dashboard showing store statistics:

  • Total sales and orders count
  • Product and customer totals
  • Last sync timestamps
  • Store connection status (Live/Paused/Error)

Auto Sync

Auto Sync Enabled: Any changes made in Odoo are automatically pushed to Shopify. Toggle this feature per record as needed.

Multi-Store Support

Connect and manage multiple Shopify stores from a single Odoo instance. Each store has independent sync settings and tracking.
